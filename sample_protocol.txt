Clinical Trial Protocol: CT-SAMPLE-101

1. Eligibility:
- Patients must be 18 to 65 years old.
- Must have a BMI between 18.5 and 29.9.

2. Dosing:
- The required dose is 50 mg administered orally once daily.
- A missed dose must not be replaced by a double dose.

3. Monitoring:
- Blood pressure must be measured at every visit.
- Any severe adverse event (SAE) must be reported within 24 hours.
